## Early Diagnosis

- The account is growing, not literally stagnant: approximately +41.7% over six months based on the user's rounded history.
- Growth appears to have decelerated: the latest 90 days added 1,344 net followers, while the six-month estimate implies a much faster preceding period. This is directional because the 12,000 starting value is approximate.
- The largest paid-assisted growth mechanism visible is not generic tattoo process footage. It is Amelie's face plus a clear tension/curiosity line around desirable but unclaimed original designs. It must be reproduced without boosting before it can be called an organic winner.
- Static/carousel design and body-placement content currently creates more aggregate views and interactions than Reels, although posting volume by format is unknown.
- Typical Reels reach 51-76% non-followers but convert weakly. Discovery is occurring; the larger bottleneck is converting attention into profile visits and follows.
- Copenhagen should be part of the story and collaboration network, not the total addressable audience. The existing audience is already European and distributed.
- The likely moat is the combination of recognizable moving-body linework, Amelie as an on-camera artist, a German-to-Copenhagen creative-life story, and the process of turning human movement/identity into one-off wearable art.

- TikTok is newly active with approximately 20 followers and too little history for a useful baseline.
- Exact allocation within the confirmed 6-10 weekly growth hours.
- Whether clients can be filmed and whether reactions or personal stories can be used with written consent.
- Story-level analytics, repeat-viewer data, profile visit sources, and gross follows/unfollows.
- Which posts drove the six-month jump from about 12,000 to 17,000.
- Amelie's strongest personal interests and opinions beyond tattooing, needed to keep personality content authentic.
- Allocation of the confirmed EUR 250-750 monthly test budget.

## Assumptions Until Confirmed

- Amelie can allocate 6-10 hours per week, including one batch-filming block and one analytics/outreach block.
- Client privacy and informed consent take priority over content capture.
- The two 332/181-follow face Reels are treated as paid-assisted until proven otherwise. No other post is classified from the German `Bewerben` action alone.
- Art and creative process are the only non-tattoo personal-brand topics currently confirmed as naturally authentic. Fashion, relationships, and Copenhagen lifestyle should enter through collaborators or artwork rather than forced lifestyle posting.
- Follower counts and platform metrics from public third parties are snapshots, not stable facts.

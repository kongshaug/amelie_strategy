## Operating Strategy

### Positioning

Use an English-first European creator position: contemporary linework and dancing figures, explained by the artist who turns movement, identity, and personal ideas into one-off art for the body. Copenhagen is a recognizable setting and collaboration network, not the limit of the audience. Add accurate English subtitles to every spoken video. German is a controlled test, not the default language.

### Content Mix

| Share | Content job | Repeatable subjects |
|---:|---|---|
| 40% | High-probability discovery | Face-led available-design tension, one-off design reveal, placement choice |
| 30% | Art and authority | Sketch-to-body transformation, why a line moves this way, healed result, design decisions |
| 20% | Personality and repeat viewing | Artist viewpoint, German-to-Copenhagen creative story, honest studio decisions |
| 10% | Community and borrowed reach | Client story with written consent, studio/artist Collab post, event-created concept |

Do not force generic fashion, relationship, or lifestyle content. Use those worlds only through authentic art or collaborator overlap.

### Measurement Definitions

Record results at 24 hours, 72 hours, and 7 days. Use the 7-day organic result for decisions.

| KPI | Formula or rule |
|---|---|
| Follows per 1,000 views | Attributed follows / views x 1,000 |
| Profile visits per 1,000 views | Profile visits / views x 1,000 |
| Profile-to-follow rate | Attributed follows / profile visits |
| Non-follower share | Non-follower views or reach / total views or reach |
| Share rate | Sends plus reposts / views |
| Save rate | Saves / views |
| Weekly net adds | End-of-week followers minus prior end-of-week followers |
| Organic result | Metrics captured before any promotion begins |

The two historical face-led available-design Reels with 332 and 181 attributed follows are likely paid-assisted. They establish a promising creative hypothesis only. They must not be described as organic winners, used as an organic benchmark, or promoted again until the format passes the organic validation gate below.

### Batch Capture Checklist

One batch should produce three short-form masters and one carousel:

1. Record three different first-second openings: face-led tension, finished result first, and moving sketch first.
2. Capture clean vertical clips of face, hand drawing, stencil, placement, tattoo process, final movement, and one direct-to-camera explanation.
3. Record each spoken line twice and keep the strongest natural delivery.
4. Capture 6-10 still frames for the carousel: design, 2-3 placements, detail, result, and artist note.
5. Get written client consent before filming, publishing a story, or identifying a client. If consent is absent, use Amelie, paper, practice material, or anonymous close-ups.
6. Export a clean master, then make separate Instagram and TikTok openings, text, captions, and audio choices.

## Controlled Experiment Rules

1. Change one variable per matched pair: opening, duration, language treatment, cover, CTA, or collaborator distribution.
2. Hold subject, approximate length, posting window, caption purpose, and editing pace as constant as practical.
3. Use the same Tuesday/Thursday publishing window for matched Instagram tests. If Trial Reels is available, it may reduce audience contamination, but log that distribution separately.
4. Do not add paid distribution until every required 7-day organic snapshot is recorded and the applicable validation gate is complete.
5. A standard format decision requires either 3 organic executions or 10,000 combined organic views, with complete 7-day data for every included post. Before then, results are directional and may set the next replication priority, but they cannot validate, scale, or park the format.
6. Do not delete an eligible post because of weak first-day performance. Keep it live for at least 14 days unless it contains an error, privacy issue, or recommendation-eligibility problem.

### Organic Face-Reel Validation Gate

Run two matched, unboosted face-led available-design Reels in Weeks 1-2. This is the explicit exception to the standard 3-execution/10,000-combined-view rule: the format becomes organically validated after two passes only if both Reels have complete 7-day data and each reaches all four thresholds:

- At least 3,000 organic views.
- At least 55% non-follower share.
- At least 10 profile visits per 1,000 views.
- At least 1.5 attributed follows per 1,000 views.

If only one passes, label the format promising and use the face-led one-off-design Reel scheduled for Day 30 as the third unboosted replication. Wait for its complete 7-day result on 2026-10-27. The format validates only if that third Reel passes, producing two passes among the three. If neither initial Reel passes, or the mixed-result third Reel fails, retain face-led artist content as a test category but stop treating design tension as the lead growth engine. No paid test may begin while this gate is unresolved, and later paid results never count toward validation.

### After Test 2 Has Seven Days Of Data: Face-Led Validation

- Scale the face-led available-design format only if both organic tests each reach at least 3,000 organic views, at least 55% non-follower share, at least 10 profile visits per 1,000 views, and at least 1.5 attributed follows per 1,000 views at day 7.
- Run the Day 30 unboosted replication if only one passes; do not spend until its seven-day result supplies the second pass.
- Stop calling it a lead format if neither passes. The historical paid-assisted totals do not override this result.

### End Of Week 4: Select Replication Priorities

- Prioritize a third replication when the first two attempts have a median of at least 1.3 follows per 1,000 views or 15 profile visits per 1,000 views with at least 8% profile-to-follow conversion.
- Revise one variable for the third execution if the first two get at least 55% non-follower reach but under 0.5 follows per 1,000 views; discovery works, but positioning or CTA does not.
- Kill or park a standard format only after 3 executions or 10,000 combined organic views with complete seven-day data remain below 0.5 follows per 1,000 views and below 8 profile visits per 1,000 views.
- Do not use likes alone to keep a format.

### End Of Week 8: Scale Eligibility

- Give 70% of output to a format only after at least 3 organic posts produce a median of 1.5 follows per 1,000 views and 10 profile visits per 1,000 views.
- Require at least 55% non-follower reach and no single outlier producing more than 60% of the format's follows.
- Keep 20% for adjacent variants and 10% for a new bet to avoid creative fatigue.

### After Post 30 Has Seven Days Of Data: TikTok Gate

- Scale to 3 posts/week through December if the 30-post median is at least 750 views, 5 posts exceed 2,000 views, or TikTok adds at least 75 followers.
- Maintain a low-effort 2 posts/week test if the median is 500-749 views, at least 3 posts exceed 2,000, or TikTok adds 30-74 followers.
- Pause for four weeks if the median is below 500, fewer than 3 posts exceed 2,000, and fewer than 30 followers are added, after confirming the posts are eligible for the For You feed.
- No paid TikTok promotion during the 30-post pilot.

### Final Gate: January Allocation

- Continue only the top 2 organic formats, the top collaboration type, and TikTok if its gate passed.
- Rank formats by median follows per 1,000 views, then profile visits per 1,000, then non-follower share. Use watch time, shares, and saves to diagnose why, not to replace follower conversion.
- Separate organic follows, paid-attributed follows, and account-level net growth in the final report.
- If core growth is missed but content conversion improved, keep the winner and fix output consistency or distribution. If views rise but follows per 1,000 fall, tighten positioning before adding volume or spend.

## Paid Distribution Rules

The available range is EUR 250-750 per month, but it is a ceiling, not a requirement. Unvalidated creative should leave money unspent.

### Entry Rules

1. Spend EUR 0 on the Week 1-2 face-validation posts until both have complete 7-day organic snapshots.
2. A post may enter paid testing only if it reaches at least 1.5 organic follows per 1,000 views, 10 profile visits per 1,000 views, and 55% non-follower reach within 7 days.
3. Require two organic passes for the face-led format before any paid test. If the initial pair is mixed, the third replication must supply the second pass before spend begins; merely scheduling it is insufficient.
4. Do not promote website announcements, generic travel recaps, booking schedules, or thank-you Reels for follower growth. Their documented follower conversion is weak or serves another objective.
5. Use profile visits as the campaign objective when Meta makes it available and reports it clearly. Otherwise use the closest measurable engagement/video objective, but judge the test on attributed Instagram follows and cost per follow.

### Monthly Allocation

| Monthly ceiling | Initial tests | Winner scaling | Reserve | Rule |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| EUR 250 | 2 x EUR 50 | EUR 125 | EUR 25 | Run the second test only after the first has usable follow attribution. |
| EUR 500 | 3 x EUR 60 | EUR 270 | EUR 50 | Test creative first, then audience; never change both in one cell. |
| EUR 750 | 3 x EUR 75 | EUR 450 | EUR 75 | Put at least 60% of deployed spend behind one proven winner, not many small boosts. |

If fewer qualified creatives exist, do not redeploy the unused test money. Reserve may cover one retest or remain unspent; it is not an invitation to boost a weak post.

### Audience And Stop Rules

- First paid cell: ages 18-34 in Germany, Denmark, France, and Sweden, using English creative. This reflects the documented audience rather than a Copenhagen-only assumption.
- Keep creative constant when testing a second geography or audience. Keep audience constant when testing creative.
- Capture the organic screenshot immediately before promotion and log paid spend, paid reach, profile visits, attributed follows, and cost per attributed follow separately.
- Stop at EUR 25 if tracking shows zero attributed follows or the objective is not producing measurable profile activity.
- Stop a cell at EUR 50 if cost per attributed follow is above EUR 2.00. A cell at EUR 1.51-2.00 may finish its test but does not scale automatically.
- Scale only at EUR 1.50 or less per attributed follow and only while the 7-day profile-to-follow rate remains at least 5%.
- Increase a winning daily budget by no more than 25% every 48 hours. Cap one creative at EUR 150 before a fresh review to limit fatigue and false confidence.
- Pause if cost per follow worsens by 30% from the prior 3-day average or frequency rises while follower conversion falls.
- Never describe paid-attributed followers as organic growth. Review account net growth as the final reality check.

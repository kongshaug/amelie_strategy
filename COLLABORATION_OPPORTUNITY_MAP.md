# Amelie Anders Collaboration Opportunity Map

Research date: 2026-09-19

Primary objective: acquire relevant Instagram followers. Bookings, sales, event attendance, press mentions, and website traffic are secondary unless they produce measurable discovery, profile visits, and follows.

This map expands section 12 of `AMELIE_AUDIENCE_GROWTH_STRATEGY.md`. Source IDs in brackets resolve to `RESEARCH_SOURCES.md`. Direct links in this document were checked on the research date. Public follower counts are dated snapshots, not stable facts. Every future event date is marked `RECHECK BEFORE ACTION`; reconfirm the date, application status, fee, access, filming permission, and publishing commitment before spending time or money.

## Decision Basis

### Evidence

- Amelie had 17,322 Instagram followers on 2026-09-18. Her audience was 78.9% women, 85.6% age 18-34, and distributed across Germany, Denmark, France, Sweden, and the United States [A07].
- Ordinary sampled Reels reached 51.6%-75.6% non-followers but usually generated only 0-10 follows. Discovery exists; follower conversion is the larger constraint [A07].
- Earlier collaboration Reels with Kult Tattoo Kollektiv and Studio Spiced generated only three and two follows. A collaborator tag or shared location is not enough [A07].
- Amelie's strongest current creative hypothesis combines her face, an unresolved design question, recognizable moving-body linework, and a reason to return. The two largest examples were likely paid-assisted and must not be presented as organic proof [A07, A08].
- Instagram Collabs can include up to three collaborators, allowing one artifact to appear across relevant accounts when every party accepts the invitation [P05].

### Operating Inference

The best partner is not necessarily the largest account. The best partner has a relevant women-led, art, design, body, fashion, queer, music, or tattoo audience; can create a genuine artifact with Amelie; will publish to its own feed; and gives viewers a specific reason to follow Amelie for the next episode.

### Required Transfer Structure

Every collaboration must contain all six elements:

1. A jointly interesting artifact, not a networking recap.
2. Both parties or both visual worlds in the first three seconds or first carousel slide.
3. Partner-side feed publishing, preferably an accepted Instagram Collab, plus a same-day Story.
4. An unresolved next step hosted by Amelie: vote, final placement, tattoo result, healed result, or next interpretation.
5. An Amelie-specific follow line, such as "Follow @amelieanders.tattoo to see which body this drawing finds."
6. A seven-day scorecard covering non-follower reach, profile visits, attributed follows, follows per 1,000 views, partner reshares, and any unique QR activity.

Workload rule: Plan any activation over 4 hours across 2-4 weeks. In each affected week, activation work replaces rather than adds to that week's Reel/filming/outreach blocks. Run a maximum of one complex activation per month, and skip the activation if core publishing would exceed 10 hours in any affected week.

Do not exchange a free tattoo for an unmeasured mention. Do not accept "we will tag you" in place of feed distribution. Do not attend an event without a participant role, capture plan, publishing commitment, and measurable handoff.

## Measurement And Decision Rules

### Before Publication

1. Record Amelie's follower count, prior 28-day average daily net adds, profile visits, and the median performance of the last four comparable posts.
2. Save the partner's shared audience and prior Collab screenshots with the date.
3. Write down the agreed publisher, Collab accounts, Stories, pin duration, post time, CTA, QR/link codes, and expected partner Insights delivery.
4. Define one primary KPI. Default: organic attributed follows per 1,000 views. For offline activations, add unique QR opens but do not replace the follow KPI.
5. Do not boost during the seven-day organic observation window.

### Snapshot Timing

| Time | Record | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Immediately before post | Followers, baseline metrics, accepted Collab status | Fixed starting point |
| 24 hours | Views/reach, non-follower share, profile visits, follows, shares, saves, Story reshares | Detect delivery or setup failures; do not declare a winner |
| 72 hours | Same metrics plus partner Story/feed completion and QR opens | Midpoint diagnostic |
| 7 days | Full organic post metrics and account-level net adds | Primary decision point |
| 14 or 21 days | Long-tail follows, follow-up episode, press/QR residual | Final target-specific upside assessment |

### Core Formulas

```text
Attributed follows per 1,000 views = attributed follows / views x 1,000
Profile visits per 1,000 views = profile visits / views x 1,000
Profile-to-follow rate = attributed follows / profile visits
Non-follower share = non-follower reach or views / total reach or views
QR open rate = unique QR opens / physical participants or distributed units
Directional net lift = actual account net adds - expected net adds from baseline
```

Keep post-attributed gross follows separate from account-level net growth. `Directional net lift` is not causal proof because other content and external events continue during the window.

### Repeat, Scale, Or Stop

- **Repeat:** At least 1.5 attributed follows per 1,000 views and at least 1.25 times the matched-post follow rate, with relevant comments/shares and all partner deliverables completed.
- **Scale:** Two executions meet the repeat rule; at least one also reaches 50% non-followers and 25 incremental follows. Then repeat the mechanism with a second partner, not merely the same aesthetic.
- **Promising but under-distributed:** Follow conversion is strong but total reach is small. Repeat once with a better hook or primary publisher before killing.
- **Redesign:** The post reaches non-followers but generates fewer than 0.5 follows per 1,000 views after at least 10,000 views. Change premise, follow promise, or profile handoff.
- **Stop:** The partner misses feed/Story commitments, refuses Insights, the concept creates safety/rights concerns, or two properly distributed attempts fail to beat matched baseline.
- **Offline stop:** Remove a physical asset after two weeks or at event close. Do not keep printing if unique opens and follow indicators are negligible.
- **PR stop:** Record article/social publication separately. Do not call page views, estimated readership, or press prestige follower growth.

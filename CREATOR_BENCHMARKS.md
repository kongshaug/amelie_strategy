# Creator Benchmarks

Benchmark date: 2026-09-19

Purpose: compare relevant tattoo creators for Amelie Anders' follower-growth strategy. This is an attention and follow-conversion benchmark, not a booking or revenue ranking.

## Method And Limits

- `Snapshot` means an approximate, point-in-time public profile count researched on 2026-09-19. It is not a live count and must be rechecked before external use.
- The Instagram snapshots below are directional. They do not prove growth rate, engagement quality, organic reach, or follower acquisition. No creator is described as currently growing rapidly because historical trend data was not available.
- `Evidence` means a fact documented by a cited profile, first-party page, interview, or project report. A visually apparent portfolio characteristic is identified as an observation rather than an analytics claim.
- `Inference` means strategic interpretation from the documented work. It is not a statement from the creator and not proof of audience behavior.
- `Best-performing` is used only where public performance evidence exists. Otherwise the entry names a `standout project` and explicitly says that a top-performing social post was not verified.
- `Posting frequency` refers to social posting cadence. It is `not verified` for every profile because no consistent dated-post audit was available. Website or blog activity is not substituted for social frequency.
- Instagram is the common comparison platform. Oozy is the only creator in this set with a verified TikTok snapshot in the research record. No TikTok audience counts are inferred for anyone else.
- Sources use IDs from `RESEARCH_SOURCES.md` where available. Additional first-party and profile sources are listed at the end.

## Individual Benchmarks

### Daisy Watson

- **Region/base - Evidence:** Australia-born and described as Berlin-based in 2023. Her work is connected to Berlin nightlife, music labels, parties and art spaces. [C01]
- **Platform - Snapshot:** Instagram [@daisydoestattoos](https://www.instagram.com/daisydoestattoos/), approximately **139.3K followers** on 2026-09-19. Her broader art identity also appears at [@daisy___watson](https://www.instagram.com/daisy___watson/). [S01] [C01]
- **Tattoo style - Evidence:** Joyful naive-art linework with recurring flowers, psychedelic swirls, barbed-wire hearts, dancing figures and queer symbolism. Watson says line is the through-line across her media and describes the work as connection, fun but deep, and not too serious. [C01] [C02]
- **Content style - Evidence/observation:** Tattoo outcomes sit beside silk painting, jewelry, clothing, album artwork, party graphics and personal artistic process. The documented content world is multidisciplinary rather than a tattoo-only catalogue. [C01] [C02]
- **Best-performing or standout - Evidence:** No top social post was verified. The standout documented project is `Thoughts and Feelings`, an intimate, diary-like collection painted on silk and exhibited in Berlin. Its material, wordplay, queer imagery and stories from friends made the same motifs work as an exhibition. [C01]
- **Posting frequency:** not verified.
- **Personality/voice - Evidence:** Spontaneous, curious, candid and community-oriented. She describes trusting what speaks to a person aesthetically, finding beauty in mistakes, and moving between softness and strength. [C01]
- **Hooks - Inference:** A familiar motif moved onto an unexpected object; a line or in-joke turned into an artwork; the contrast of a swirl and a spike; a new medium learned in public.
- **Storytelling - Evidence:** Intimate anecdotes, lyrics, jokes and friendships feed the work. The flower motif also carries childhood and family associations. [C01]
- **Editing - Unknown:** No repeatable social-video editing pattern or retention data was verified.
- **Trends - Evidence/unknown:** The work participates in queer art, nightlife and multidisciplinary design culture, but no verified trend-audio or reactive social strategy was found.
- **Collaborations - Evidence:** Jewelry with YCCIJ, artwork for music labels and party collectives, and visual work across clothing and nightlife spaces. Collaboration performance is unknown. [C01] [C02]
- **Shareability - Inference:** Simple symbols are readable at thumbnail scale, while witty or intimate meanings give people a reason to send them to a friend. Moving the motifs between tattoo, silk, jewelry and music creates cross-audience entry points.
- **Why people follow - Inference:** They can follow a recognizable creative world and Berlin cultural identity, not just wait for another healed tattoo photograph.
- **Transferable lesson for Amelie:** Treat Amelie's established motifs as portable intellectual property. A recurring Amelie symbol could become a drawing diary, textile, poster, Copenhagen installation or fashion collaboration while remaining recognizably hers.
- **Sources:** [C01], [C02], [S01].

### Mo Ganji

- **Region/base - Evidence:** Berlin-based, with documented guest spots in London, New York, Seattle and Los Angeles. [O02]
- **Platform - Snapshot:** Instagram [@moganji](https://www.instagram.com/moganji/), approximately **353.2K followers** on 2026-09-19. [S02]
- **Tattoo style - Evidence:** Minimalist fine-line images made with a single continuous line, often depicting animals, people and abstract forms. The stated intent is to remove unnecessary detail so meaning becomes central. [O02]
- **Content style - Evidence/observation:** Minimal tattoo and drawing showcases, client meaning, process philosophy, and project-led storytelling. The constraint makes the result easy to understand in a still image or reveal. [C03] [C04] [O02]
- **Best-performing or standout - Evidence:** No top social post was verified. `World Piece` is the standout: one continuous design divided among 61 people from 61 countries. Momondo reports roughly 6,550 applications and documents a campaign film, participant portraits and stories. This verifies project scale, not Mo's social-post performance. [C03] [C04]
- **Posting frequency:** not verified.
- **Personality/voice - Evidence:** Calm, deliberate, reflective and meaning-led; his first-party biography describes an unrushed, collaborative tattoo process. [O02]
- **Hooks - Inference:** "One image, one unbroken line" is an immediate creative constraint. For `World Piece`, the stronger hook is that separate strangers carry one connected artwork.
- **Storytelling - Evidence:** Designs are framed around personal meaning and, in the campaign, common humanity across borders. Participant stories were matched to individual line segments. [C03] [C04]
- **Editing - Evidence/unknown:** The `World Piece` film had documented professional production and editing partners. Mo's normal social editing pattern is unknown. [C04]
- **Trends - Unknown:** No evidence of a trend-led posting strategy was found.
- **Collaborations - Evidence:** Momondo, Tattoodo, Smuggler, creative agency &Co, a film director and a portrait photographer contributed to `World Piece`. [C04]
- **Shareability - Inference:** The style can be explained in seconds, redrawn mentally, and attached to universal meaning. The project adds a scale and unity story that press and non-tattoo audiences can retell.
- **Why people follow - Inference:** For a distinctive design rule, emotional restraint and the anticipation of how the next idea will be solved with one line.
- **Transferable lesson for Amelie:** Define a simple public-facing rule for one recurring series. A clear constraint can make Amelie's work easier to remember, explain and share without simplifying her whole practice.
- **Sources:** [C03], [C04], [O02], [S02].

## Synthesis

### What The Evidence Actually Shows

1. **Recognition precedes format.** The clearest benchmarks have a visual idea that can be named quickly: one continuous line, narrow vertical worlds, blurry/3D fine line, occult hand-poke symbols, or animation/anatomy mashups. A Reel can distribute that idea, but the format does not substitute for it.
2. **The strongest standout projects are larger than routine portfolio posts.** `World Piece`, `Thoughts and Feelings`, `TATI`, `OOZYBOWL`, Vism Studio and cross-category design collaborations give press, collaborators and non-tattoo audiences a reason to carry the story.
3. **Several follow propositions extend beyond tattoo outcomes.** Mira adds beauty and candid personal perspective; Daisy adds art and nightlife; Dr. Woo adds luxury and fashion; Lauren adds celebrity stories and practical guidance; Yatzil adds travel and entrepreneur identity.
4. **Cultural specificity travels.** Daldam's Korean visual language, Oozy and Sol's Korean-to-Los Angeles context, Daisy's queer Berlin world and Lauren's Sydney-based celebrity practice are not weakened by being specific. Specificity gives global viewers something memorable to enter.
5. **Collaboration works best when it produces a new story or object.** A shared audience alone is not the strongest evidence here. The useful examples create a global connected artwork, a watch, a book, a bowl, a studio or a celebrity-linked cultural moment.
6. **Personality does not require constant talking-head content.** It can appear as candor, restraint, philosophy, humor, client care, cultural curation or an artist's choice of projects. Amelie needs a legible point of view, not a borrowed creator persona.
7. **There is no basis here for cadence imitation.** Social posting frequency, non-follower reach, shares, saves, profile visits and follows per post were not available. The benchmark can guide creative mechanisms but cannot prove how often Amelie should publish.
8. **Audience size is not evidence of current momentum.** The snapshots range from 110.3K to 1.66M on Instagram, but there is no historical series with which to claim current rapid growth.

### Repeatable Follow Mechanisms

| Mechanism | Evidence examples | Why it may convert to follows (inference) | Application for Amelie |
|---|---|---|---|
| A one-sentence signature | Continuous line; vertical portal; blurry/3D effect | Makes the creator easy to remember and describe | Name one recurring visual series without flattening the full practice |
| Transformation and reveal | Dense mashups, miniature scenes, optical effects | Creates an open loop and a satisfying resolution | Show sketch -> placement -> unexpected finished relationship to the body |
| A world beyond skin | Silk, books, bowls, watches, studios | Gives existing motifs novelty and reaches adjacent audiences | Translate one motif into fashion, print, installation or a Copenhagen object |
| Person plus practice | Beauty candor, humor, philosophy, travel | Supplies reasons to return when no new tattoo is ready | Build one recurring personal lens tied directly to Amelie's real life |
| Cultural bridge | Korean art, Berlin nightlife, celebrity culture | Gives the post relevance to a community larger than tattoo buyers | Make Copenhagen and Amelie's own background material for universal stories |
| Project-scale collaboration | Global campaign, collective, luxury object | Combines distribution with press-worthy narrative | Prefer one high-concept collaboration over many contextless tagged posts |
| Recognizable reference | Animation, film, celebrity or symbolic archetype | Lowers comprehension time and encourages sending to a specific friend | Use references selectively and transform them through Amelie's visual language |

### Priority Lessons For Amelie

1. **Create a named signature series.** It needs a visually obvious rule, a repeatable reveal and enough range for at least 20 executions.
2. **Add a person-level reason to follow.** Select one authentic lens such as the move to Copenhagen, creative process, fashion, relationships to the body or candid artist life. Test it rather than assuming it will work.
3. **Build one project larger than an appointment.** A small exhibition, public artwork, wearable object or connected multi-person tattoo concept should be designed for collaboration and documentation from the start.
4. **Engineer the first-second visual question.** Use scale, an unusual placement, a compositional boundary, a before/after or a motif on an unexpected surface. Do not rely on generic text hooks.
5. **Measure conversion, not resemblance.** For every tested mechanism, track non-follower reach, shares, profile visits, follows and follows per 1,000 views. Keep it only if it helps discovery become following.

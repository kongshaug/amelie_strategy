# Deep Research: Amelie Anders Tattoo – Follower Growth Strategy

Your ONLY primary objective is:

# SIGNIFICANTLY INCREASE AMELIE'S FOLLOWER COUNT

Do NOT optimize primarily for bookings, revenue, website traffic or customers.

Bookings may be mentioned where relevant, but they are secondary.

The goal is to turn Amelie from a tattoo artist with a relatively stagnant social following into a **rapidly growing tattoo creator / visual artist / personality with a recognizable online presence**.

# Context

Amelie Anders is a tattoo artist who moved to Copenhagen approximately 6 months ago.

Her follower growth has stagnated.

She already has an established tattoo style, portfolio and visual identity, but her current social media presence is not generating enough new followers.

We want to understand:

> What would it take to create a SIGNIFICANT increase in followers over the next 3–6 months?

Do not give generic advice.

I want a strategy that could realistically produce **2x, 3x or potentially more follower growth**, depending on execution and content-market fit.

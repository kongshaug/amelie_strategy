# 30-Day Content Calendar

Period: Monday 2026-09-21 through Tuesday 2026-10-20

Primary objective: turn non-follower attention into profile visits, follows, repeat viewing, and community. Bookings are secondary.

## Operating Rules

- Publish in English. Add accurate English on-screen captions to every spoken video.
- Use Instagram as the primary platform. Export one clean 9:16 master without a watermark and post every planned Reel natively to the new TikTok account. Post only the Reels marked for YouTube Shorts.
- Default feed posting time is 18:30 Copenhagen time. Post the TikTok version at 19:00. Post a selected Short at 19:15. Keep these times stable during the test unless account Insights show a clearly stronger existing audience window.
- Keep Reels to roughly 10-18 seconds unless the story genuinely needs longer. Put the exact hook on screen in the first frame and say it immediately when the format is face-led.
- Use a simple visual system: Amelie's face or the finished visual first, large high-contrast hook text, close crops, no slow introduction, and a final frame that explains why to follow.
- Reply to early comments for 15 minutes after publishing, then leave the app. Community blocks later in the calendar handle the rest.
- Get written consent before showing a client's face, body, story, stencil session, or reaction. Use Amelie, paper, an iPad, mannequin templates, or consented archive material as the fallback.
- Do not turn Stories into a second production job. Use 2-4 direct frames, polls, question boxes, reposts, and raw process clips.
- Do not use a booking CTA as the lead CTA. If a design is available, state that once at the end of the caption after the follower or conversation CTA.

## Why This Mix

- The account's strongest follower-acquisition candidate is Amelie's face plus tension around original unclaimed designs, but the two known examples were likely paid-assisted. This calendar tests that concept organically before any paid use.
- Existing ordinary Reels already reach many non-followers but convert weakly. The Reels below therefore make the artistic idea and reason to follow explicit rather than showing generic process footage.
- Design, placement, and body-image carousels already produce strong reach and interactions. Each full week retains one carousel rather than switching to an all-video plan.
- The audience is mostly women aged 18-34 and is distributed across Germany, Denmark, France, Sweden, the United States, and elsewhere. The creative is English-first and European, with Copenhagen used as context rather than the audience limit.
- TikTok has about 20 followers and no reliable baseline. It receives clean native cross-posts plus four low-effort native posts made from shared footage, not separate productions. Shorts receives only the clearest visual stories.

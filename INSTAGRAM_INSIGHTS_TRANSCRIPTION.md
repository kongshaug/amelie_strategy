## Evidence-Based Observations From The Screenshots

1. Face-led design tension is the clearest paid-assisted follower-acquisition candidate. The two visible face-led unclaimed-design Reels generated 513 follows from 81,815 views, or 6.27 follows per 1,000 views combined, but both were likely boosted.
2. The best face-led design Reel converted about 19 times more efficiently than the median of the ten fully documented ordinary Reels: 7.95 follows per 1,000 views versus approximately 0.42. Because the Reel was likely paid-assisted, the creative should be rerun unboosted before attributing this lift to the format.
3. The top two visible 30-day posts generated 228 of at least 255 visible attributed follows, or 89.4%. Performance is highly concentrated.
4. Ordinary Reels already reach non-followers: the documented range is 51.6% to 75.6%. Their main problem is weak follow conversion, not complete failure to leave the follower base.
5. "I do abstract linework and dancing figures" is the strongest fully documented ordinary Reel: 10 follows, 97 profile visits, 67.5% non-followers, and 1.53 follows per 1,000 views.
6. "Trampstamps are so back" has the longest shown average watch time at 10 seconds, but Reel duration is missing, so completion cannot be compared.
7. Collaborations improved local audience concentration more clearly than follower conversion. The Kult Collab reached 40.5% Denmark and generated 3 follows; Studio Spiced reached 25.5% Denmark and generated 2.
8. Guest-spot and thank-you Reels produce weak follower conversion. They are useful for clients and relationships but should be secondary in a follower-growth feed.
9. The audience is unusually clear: women 18-34 are 85.6% of followers. Germany is larger than Denmark, and France/Sweden are meaningful, supporting English-first European positioning rather than Copenhagen-only targeting.
10. Posts/carousels dominate total recent views and interactions. Design sheets, placement ideas, and strong body-placement photos are important growth assets, not merely portfolio filler.

## Data Limitations

- Reel duration and completion rate were not supplied.
- Sends/shares show `--` in many individual Reel screens even when the 30-day ranking shows paper-plane counts. Instagram may define or expose these fields differently in each view.
- The `Bewerben` interface label does not prove a post was boosted. The user's later recollection, not the button, is why the two face-led Reels are classified as likely paid-assisted.
- Approximate boost spend was EUR 10/day for about two weeks, but it is unknown whether that was per Reel or combined. Ad objective, targeting, paid reach, and paid follow attribution are unavailable.
- Top-content follower counts are gross post attribution; the 90-day +1,344 is net account growth after unfollows.
- Story frequency, post frequency, exact publishing dates, reach by individual post, and ad spend were not shown.
- Screenshot totals by content type do not sum to the account total, so they should be used as displayed rather than forced into a complete allocation.

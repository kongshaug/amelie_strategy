# Growth Experiments, KPIs, and Follower Model

Date: 2026-09-19

## Objective and decision frame

The primary objective is net follower growth. Bookings, revenue, website traffic, and bio-link taps are secondary and must not displace content that acquires followers.

This document starts at 17,322 Instagram followers and uses first-party Instagram Insights as the controlling evidence. Over the latest 90 days, the account generated 1,052,204 views, 25,692 profile visits, and 1,344 net followers. That equals about 350,735 views and 448 net followers per month, 1.28 net follows per 1,000 views, and 5.23 net follows per 100 profile visits [A07]. Ordinary documented Reels reached 51.6%-75.6% non-followers, so discovery already occurs; weak conversion from attention to follow is the more important constraint.

The two strongest face-led available-design Reels generated 332 and 181 attributed follows, or 7.95 and 4.52 gross follows per 1,000 views. They were likely boosted, but spend allocation and paid versus organic attribution are unknown [A07, A08]. They are a high-value lead, not proven organic winners. An available-design placement carousel generated 47 follows and 1.15 gross follows per 1,000 views. The best fully documented ordinary Reel generated 1.53; the median among the ten ordinary documented Reels was about 0.42 gross follows per 1,000 views [A07].

## Experiment conventions

- The list below contains exactly 30 experiments, ranked in execution order.
- Tier 1 means extremely high potential, Tier 2 high potential, Tier 3 medium potential, and Tier 4 low potential.
- `Expected incremental follower upside` means followers above the matched baseline during the stated test, not total attributed follows. It is a planning range, not a promise.
- Follower upside means Instagram followers unless the field explicitly says native TikTok, YouTube, or Pinterest followers.
- Direct cost excludes Amelie's labor but includes production, media, entry, and partner fees. Do not assign a cash value to bookings.
- Compare content after the same elapsed time, normally seven days. Record 24-hour and seven-day results separately.
- A threshold does not override judgment when distribution is too small. Unless a test specifies otherwise, require three executions or 10,000 combined views before killing a format.

## Ranked follower-growth experiments

### 1. Unboosted face-led design-tension replication

- **Tier/rank:** Tier 1 - extremely high potential; rank 1 of 30.
- **Hypothesis:** Amelie's face plus genuine tension around an unclaimed one-off design drives materially more follows than a design-only Reel even without paid distribution.
- **Exact action:** Publish three 8-15 second Reels, one per week, opening on Amelie saying a distinct line such as "I drew this for someone I have not met yet." Cut within two seconds to the design, show two body-placement mockups, return to her face, and close with "Follow to see where it lands." Do not boost any version during the test.
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels; use Trial Reels for one version if available.
- **Time:** 2.5 hours to batch three Reels plus 20 minutes per post.
- **Cost:** EUR 0-30.
- **Expected incremental follower upside:** 50-180 Instagram followers over three weeks.
- **KPI:** Gross follows per 1,000 views, supported by non-follower reach and profile visits per 1,000 views.
- **Duration:** 21 days; score each Reel at day 7.
- **Success threshold:** Combined gross follows per 1,000 views >=2.5 and at least 25 incremental follows versus three matched ordinary Reels.
- **Failure threshold:** Combined gross follows per 1,000 views <0.75 after >=10,000 views, or zero incremental follows versus the matched baseline.

### 2. Movement-to-body transformation series

- **Tier/rank:** Tier 1 - extremely high potential; rank 2 of 30.
- **Hypothesis:** Showing a real movement become a recognizable line drawing and then a tattoo gives the work a repeatable story mechanism that increases shares and follows.
- **Exact action:** Make four 12-20 second episodes with the same structure: one-second movement clip, freeze-frame trace, sketch refinement, stencil/body placement, finished tattoo, and series card "Movement 01/04." Use consenting clients or Amelie for movement footage.
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels, then native edits on TikTok and YouTube Shorts.
- **Time:** 3 hours setup plus 60-90 minutes per episode.
- **Cost:** EUR 0-80.
- **Expected incremental follower upside:** 60-240 Instagram followers over four weeks; cross-platform followers are reported separately.
- **KPI:** Instagram follows per 1,000 views; secondary KPI is shares per 1,000 views.
- **Duration:** 28 days.
- **Success threshold:** At least two of four episodes reach >=1.9 gross follows per 1,000 views, which is about 1.5x the 90-day account net rate, and series follow conversion does not decline by more than 25% from episode one to four.
- **Failure threshold:** All four episodes remain <0.75 gross follows per 1,000 views after >=20,000 combined views and shares are below matched-Reel median.

### 3. Available-design placement carousel franchise

- **Tier/rank:** Tier 1 - extremely high potential; rank 3 of 30.
- **Hypothesis:** Swipeable placement choices make original designs easier to imagine and save, producing more follows than generic portfolio carousels.
- **Exact action:** Publish four seven-slide carousels. Slide 1 is one strong design and "Where should this live?" Slides 2-5 show distinct body placements, slide 6 shows Amelie's drawing detail or face, and slide 7 says "Follow for the final placement." Keep design count and visual treatment constant.
- **Platform:** Instagram feed/carousels.
- **Time:** 75 minutes per carousel after one reusable template is built.
- **Cost:** EUR 0-40.
- **Expected incremental follower upside:** 35-140 Instagram followers over four weeks.
- **KPI:** Follows per 1,000 views; secondary KPIs are saves and shares per 1,000 views.
- **Duration:** 28 days.
- **Success threshold:** Portfolio average >=1.5 gross follows per 1,000 views and >=1.25x the follow rate of four recent portfolio carousels.
- **Failure threshold:** Portfolio average <0.75 gross follows per 1,000 views after >=20,000 combined views with no save-rate lift.

### 4. Face-led design-origin story

- **Tier/rank:** Tier 1 - extremely high potential; rank 4 of 30.
- **Hypothesis:** A specific emotional or artistic origin gives an abstract design meaning and makes viewers follow the artist, not only admire the image.
- **Exact action:** Create three 20-30 second Reels. Open with the finished drawing and a precise line such as "This figure came from the feeling of trying not to take up space." Show Amelie explaining, two sketch stages, and the final body placement. End with the next episode's unresolved question.
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels and TikTok native upload.
- **Time:** 2 hours to script and batch, then 75 minutes per edit.
- **Cost:** EUR 0-50.
- **Expected incremental follower upside:** 30-150 Instagram followers over three weeks.
- **KPI:** Follows per 1,000 views and profile visits per 1,000 views.
- **Duration:** 21 days.
- **Success threshold:** Average >=1.9 follows per 1,000 views or >=18 profile visits per 1,000 views with profile-visit-to-follow conversion >=8%.
- **Failure threshold:** Average <0.75 follows per 1,000 views and profile visits below 10 per 1,000 after >=10,000 combined views.

### 5. Dancer or movement-artist Collab Reel

- **Tier/rank:** Tier 1 - extremely high potential; rank 5 of 30.
- **Hypothesis:** A dancer's movement is unusually aligned with Amelie's visual language, so a jointly published transformation will transfer more relevant followers than a studio-only collaboration.
- **Exact action:** Select one Copenhagen dancer or movement creator only after reviewing audience fit. Film their signature movement, Amelie drawing from it, placement on the body, and the creator's reaction. Publish as an Instagram Collab from both accounts; both partners pin it for seven days and post a same-day Story with a follow-specific CTA.
- **Platform:** Instagram Collab Reel; native supporting TikTok posts on both accounts.
- **Time:** 2 hours outreach/pre-production, 3 hours shoot, 2 hours edit.
- **Cost:** EUR 0-200 production; no audience fee for the first test.
- **Expected incremental follower upside:** 75-350 Instagram followers over 14 days.
- **KPI:** Incremental follows, follows per 1,000 non-follower views, and collaborator-audience share of reached accounts where available.
- **Duration:** 14 days from publication.
- **Success threshold:** >=75 incremental follows and >=2.0 follows per 1,000 views, with at least 60% non-follower reach.
- **Failure threshold:** <15 incremental follows or <0.5 follows per 1,000 views after >=10,000 views.

### 6. Paid SCALE amplification of a proven organic winner

- **Tier/rank:** Tier 1 - extremely high potential; rank 6 of 30.
- **Hypothesis:** A creative already converting organically can acquire additional relevant followers efficiently when paid distribution is added without hiding organic performance.
- **Exact action:** This is a later paid SCALE experiment, used only after the shared organic validation gate and the initial Month 1 EUR 30 paid test. Before any paid test, require two matched organic replications that each reach >=3,000 views, >=55% non-follower share, >=10 profile visits per 1,000 views, and >=1.5 attributed follows per 1,000 views. If the two results are mixed, a third matched organic replication must pass all four thresholds before any paid test. The shared gate qualifies a creative for the initial Month 1 EUR 30 test; for this later scale stage, let the candidate run organically for seven days and require the stricter >=2.0 gross follows per 1,000 organic views before running a seven-day Meta test at EUR 20/day, split evenly between women 18-34 in Germany/Denmark/France/Sweden and a broad European women 18-34 audience. The >=2.0 candidate threshold and EUR 140 seven-day spend are scale criteria after the initial test, not conflicting entry criteria. Use the closest available profile/follower objective, exclude current followers, and preserve organic and paid reporting separately.
- **Platform:** Instagram via Meta Ads Manager or Instagram promotion reporting.
- **Time:** 90 minutes setup plus 15 minutes daily monitoring.
- **Cost:** EUR 140 media.
- **Expected incremental follower upside:** 70-250 Instagram followers over seven paid days.
- **KPI:** Cost per incremental follower, paid follows per 1,000 paid views, and seven-day account lift above expected organic net growth.
- **Duration:** Seven organic days plus seven paid days.
- **Success threshold:** Cost per incremental follower <=EUR 1.50, >=70 incremental followers, and audience quality remains within 10 percentage points of the target age/gender mix.
- **Failure threshold:** Cost per incremental follower >EUR 3.00 after EUR 70 spend, or profile visits occur but incremental follower lift is not detectable.

### 7. Trial Reels hook tournament

- **Tier/rank:** Tier 1 - extremely high potential; rank 7 of 30.
- **Hypothesis:** Holding the body of a Reel constant while testing the opening isolates hooks that improve non-follower distribution and follow conversion.
- **Exact action:** Use one 12-second movement-to-design edit and create six openings: tension, identity, surprise, opinion, question, and outcome-first. Release through Trial Reels in two batches of three where available, with identical cover, body, caption, and CTA. Promote only the best hook to the main feed.
- **Platform:** Instagram Trial Reels/main Reels.
- **Time:** 3 hours total.
- **Cost:** EUR 0.
- **Expected incremental follower upside:** 25-130 Instagram followers over 14 days.
- **KPI:** Follows per 1,000 views by hook; secondary KPI is three-second hold or closest available retention measure.
- **Duration:** 14 days.
- **Success threshold:** Winner produces >=2x the median follow rate of the other five and >=1.5 follows per 1,000 views on at least 3,000 views.
- **Failure threshold:** No hook exceeds 0.75 follows per 1,000 views or the spread between best and median is <20% after sufficient distribution.

### 8. Client identity-to-tattoo story

- **Tier/rank:** Tier 2 - high potential; rank 8 of 30.
- **Hypothesis:** A client story with a visual payoff creates emotional identification and sharing beyond tattoo audiences.
- **Exact action:** With written consent, make three 25-40 second episodes around one client sentence: "I wanted this because..." Show the person, one meaningful detail, Amelie's interpretation, and the healed or finished result. Avoid trauma bait and do not make the booking the CTA.
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels and TikTok.
- **Time:** 30 minutes added filming per client plus 2 hours per edit.
- **Cost:** EUR 0-60.
- **Expected incremental follower upside:** 35-180 Instagram followers over four weeks.
- **KPI:** Shares and follows per 1,000 views.
- **Duration:** 28 days.
- **Success threshold:** Average follows per 1,000 views >=1.5 and shares per 1,000 views >=1.5x matched process Reels.
- **Failure threshold:** Average follows per 1,000 views <0.6 with no share-rate lift after >=12,000 combined views.

### 9. Named Wabbeli character universe

- **Tier/rank:** Tier 2 - high potential; rank 9 of 30.
- **Hypothesis:** Naming recurring dancing figures and giving each a recognizable behavior creates memory, repeat viewing, and reasons to follow future episodes.
- **Exact action:** Publish six weekly episodes, each introducing one Wabbeli with a name, one personality trait, a 5-10 second animated or hand-moved behavior, and one possible tattoo placement. Number every episode and ask viewers which character should return, not which one they want to book.
- **Platform:** Instagram Reels and carousels; TikTok native versions.
- **Time:** 2-3 hours per weekly episode.
- **Cost:** EUR 0-100 total.
- **Expected incremental follower upside:** 50-260 Instagram followers over six weeks.
- **KPI:** Follows per 1,000 views and repeat-viewer/returning-audience indicators where available; comment participation is diagnostic.
- **Duration:** 42 days.
- **Success threshold:** Episodes 4-6 average >=1.5 follows per 1,000 views and at least equal episodes 1-3 on median reach, indicating the series is not decaying.
- **Failure threshold:** Episodes 4-6 are >40% below episodes 1-3 on both reach and follow rate, with average follow rate <0.75.

### 10. Profile conversion sprint

- **Tier/rank:** Tier 2 - high potential; rank 10 of 30.
- **Hypothesis:** A profile that immediately explains the moving-body art promise converts more existing profile traffic into followers.
- **Exact action:** For 14 days, set the bio first line to "I turn human movement into one-off tattoos," use a face-plus-art profile image, and pin three posts: movement transformation, face-led design story, and strongest placement carousel. Keep link and booking information below the growth promise. Compare with the prior 14 days while holding posting volume as stable as possible.
- **Platform:** Instagram profile.
- **Time:** 2 hours setup and 30 minutes analysis.
- **Cost:** EUR 0.
- **Expected incremental follower upside:** 40-150 Instagram followers over 14 days.
- **KPI:** Gross follows per 100 profile visits; secondary KPI is profile visits per 1,000 views.
- **Duration:** 14 days after a 14-day baseline is captured.
- **Success threshold:** Profile-visit-to-follow conversion >=1.25x the prior period and >=6.5%, with no >15% deterioration in profile visits per 1,000 views.
- **Failure threshold:** Conversion remains within +/-5% of baseline or falls below 4.5% with comparable traffic.

### 11. TikTok-native 30-post format sprint

- **Tier/rank:** Tier 2 - high potential; rank 11 of 30.
- **Hypothesis:** Less polished, faster, personality-led edits can find a new audience on TikTok and reveal formats worth importing to Instagram.
- **Exact action:** Publish 30 native TikToks at three per week for ten weeks, with six posts in each repeated format: movement-to-line, design opinion, client-safe story, drawing reveal, and Copenhagen artist diary. Use shared raw capture with core Instagram production, but make the first seconds, on-screen text, and captions native to TikTok. Speak English, avoid watermarked reposts, and use Creator Search Insights to choose six searchable prompts [P08, P09, P10].
- **Platform:** TikTok.
- **Time:** Cap incremental TikTok work at 60-90 minutes per week for native adaptation, publishing, and community activity; raw capture is shared with core production.
- **Cost:** EUR 0-100.
- **Expected incremental follower upside:** 30-300 native TikTok followers over ten weeks; Instagram spillover is not assumed.
- **KPI:** Native followers, native follows per 1,000 views by repeated format, portfolio median views, and number of posts above 2,000 views.
- **Duration:** Ten publishing weeks; make the final decision seven days after post 30.
- **Success threshold:** After all 30 posts each have seven days of data, either >=75 native followers or a portfolio median >=750 views with >=5 posts above 2,000 views; in either case, at least one repeated format must achieve >=2 native follows per 1,000 views.
- **Failure threshold:** After all 30 posts have seven days of data and account/post recommendation eligibility has been checked, all three are true: <30 native followers, portfolio median <500 views, and fewer than three posts above 2,000 views.

## KPI framework and formulas

### Measurement hierarchy

The decision chain is `attention -> qualified reach -> profile intent -> follows -> retained/net follows`. Views are an input, not the objective. A high-view post with weak follow conversion is not a follower-growth winner.

Use these symbols:

- `V` = total views.
- `NFV` = non-follower views.
- `PV` = profile visits.
- `GF` = gross follows attributed to content or acquired during the measurement window.
- `UF` = unfollows during the same account-level window.
- `NF` = net follows, calculated as `GF - UF`.
- `S` = shares/sends.
- `SV` = saves.
- `C3` = viewers remaining at three seconds, when available.
- `CV` = completed views, when available.
- `AWT` = average watch time in seconds.
- `L` = video length in seconds.
- `Spend` = paid media and direct partner distribution cost.
- `B` = expected baseline follows for the same volume and matched format.

Core formulas:

```text
Non-follower view rate (%) = NFV / V x 100
Profile visits per 1,000 views = PV / V x 1,000
Gross follows per 1,000 views = GF / V x 1,000
Net follows per 1,000 views = NF / V x 1,000
Profile-to-follow conversion (%) = GF / PV x 100
Share rate per 1,000 views = S / V x 1,000
Save rate per 1,000 views = SV / V x 1,000
Three-second hold rate (%) = C3 / video starts x 100
Completion rate (%) = CV / video starts x 100
Average watch ratio (%) = AWT / L x 100
Expected baseline follows = test views / 1,000 x matched-baseline follows per 1,000 views
Incremental follows = observed test follows - expected baseline follows
Follower efficiency index = test follows per 1,000 views / matched-baseline follows per 1,000 views
Cost per incremental follower = Spend / incremental follows
30-day retained incremental followers = incremental follows at day 30 versus the pre-test trend
Cost per retained follower = Spend / 30-day retained incremental followers
```

For account-level planning, use net follows per 1,000 total Instagram views because unfollows matter. For content diagnosis, use gross attributed follows per 1,000 views because Instagram does not provide post-level unfollows. Never label a gross content rate as a net rate.

## Relative keep, iterate, and kill rules

### Universal gate

Do not make a final decision from one post unless it creates a clear safety/brand issue or a decisive outlier. Normally score a format after three executions or 10,000 combined views. Compare at equal post age and against the median of ten matched posts when possible.

| Decision | Relative rule | Required interpretation | Action |
|---|---|---|---|
| Keep | Follower efficiency index >=1.5, positive incremental follows, and no audience-quality warning | The format acquires followers at least 50% better than its matched baseline | Schedule 3-5 more executions; preserve the winning mechanism |
| Scale | Index >=2.0 on at least two executions, or one execution adds >=100 incremental follows with >=2.0 follows/1,000 views | Evidence is repeatable enough to increase volume | Allocate up to 30% of next month's feed; test paid support only after organic scoring |
| Iterate | Index 0.75-1.49, or follow rate is weak but one upstream KPI is >=1.25x baseline | The idea may work but the hook, profile promise, pacing, or CTA leaks | Change exactly one variable and rerun twice |
| Kill | Index <0.75 after the universal gate and no upstream KPI is >=1.25x baseline | The mechanism consumes capacity without follower evidence | Stop the format for 60 days; retain reusable footage only |
| Immediate kill | Zero follows after >=10,000 qualified views, paid CPF >EUR 3 after half-budget, or harmful sentiment/consent issue | More exposure does not repair conversion or safety | Stop distribution immediately |

Upstream diagnosis determines the iteration:

| Signal | Likely problem | Single next change |
|---|---|---|
| Low non-follower reach, good follow rate | Packaging/distribution | Replace first frame, hook, cover, or collaboration distribution |
| High non-follower reach, low profile visits | Interest without creator curiosity | Add face, point of view, unresolved series promise, or stronger identity |
| High profile visits, low profile-to-follow conversion | Profile does not explain future value | Change bio/pins/profile image, not the content body |
| Strong watch/completion, low follows | Satisfying but interchangeable content | Add recognizable worldview, series continuity, and a follow-specific reason |
| High comments, low shares/follows | Conversation is not translating to acquisition | Narrow the prompt or stop using comments as the success signal |
| High follows, high 30-day unfollows | Wrong expectation or low-quality acquisition | Align CTA/creative with the content Amelie will continue publishing |

### Channel-specific relative rules

- **Organic:** Keep when gross follows per 1,000 views are >=1.5x the matched organic median and positive incremental follows persist across at least two posts. Do not use paid-assisted face Reels in this comparison.
- **Paid:** Keep when cost per incremental follower is <=EUR 1.50 and the 30-day retained cost is <=EUR 2.50. Iterate from EUR 1.51-3.00 if the creative first proved organic. Kill above EUR 3.00 after at least EUR 70 spend.
- **Collaboration:** Keep when follows per 1,000 views are >=1.25x the matched organic format and incremental follows are >=25, or >=75 for a major activation. Iterate if reach is strong but profile visits are weak. Kill a partner archetype after two activations each add <10 incremental follows.
- **TikTok/Shorts/Pinterest:** Judge native follower conversion first. Do not claim Instagram growth from another platform without tracked profile traffic or a measured Instagram lift.
- **Offline/PR:** Keep only when a unique link/QR, publication window, or collaborator analytics make follower transfer observable. Exposure without a measurable follow path is not a growth result.

15. Untracked flyers, stickers, and QR codes. A physical placement without a unique link and scan-to-follow measurement is decoration, not acquisition.
16. Cross-posting watermarked files without adapting the hook, title, text safe area, and pacing to the native platform.
17. Overproducing one video for eight hours before its hook or format has evidence. Spend polish on repeated winners.
18. Running all 30 experiments at once. Execute in rank order, with no more than three active acquisition tests plus one profile/measurement test.
19. Changing hook, edit, CTA, format, and audience simultaneously; the result cannot teach the next decision.
20. Treating the breakout scenario as a committed target or increasing spend simply because the organic path missed it.

## Five major bets

### Bet 1: Build a face-led design-tension franchise

- **Evidence:** Two face-led available-design Reels produced 513 attributed follows from 81,815 views, or 6.27 gross follows per 1,000 combined. That is far above ordinary documented Reels, but likely paid-assisted and not yet causally separated [A07, A08].
- **Expected impact:** Highest near-term conversion potential; planning upside of roughly 150-700 incremental Instagram followers per month after organic validation and selective paid scale, not a promise.
- **Required effort:** 3-5 hours weekly for batching, editing, design mockups, and review.
- **Execution:** Run experiment 1 unboosted, use experiment 7 to isolate hooks, retain Amelie's face plus real one-off-design tension, and publish at least one episode weekly. Add paid support only through experiment 6 after the organic threshold is met.
- **KPI:** Gross follows per 1,000 organic views first; then cost per incremental and 30-day retained follower for paid scale.

### Bet 2: Own the movement-to-body creative category

- **Evidence:** Amelie's established language is abstract linework, dancing figures, softness, collaboration, and drawings that move with the body [A01]. The account needs a recognizable mechanism, not merely good tattoo work. The Wabbeli origin material supports a named visual world [A03].
- **Expected impact:** Strongest long-term moat and repeat-viewing mechanism; planning upside of 100-600 incremental Instagram followers per month once one or two serial formats repeat.
- **Required effort:** 4-6 hours weekly, mostly shared with normal design/client documentation.
- **Execution:** Alternate movement-to-body transformations, named Wabbeli episodes, and one-design/three-body adaptations. Use fixed visual cues and episode numbering. Preserve client consent and use Amelie or collaborators when client filming is unavailable.
- **KPI:** Follows per 1,000 views across the series, share rate, and reach/follow stability from early to later episodes.

### Bet 3: Operate a hook lab and scale only repeat winners

- **Evidence:** Documented ordinary Reels already reach majority non-followers, but most convert at 0-10 follows. This means more generic publishing is less valuable than improving packaging and creator curiosity [A07]. Trial Reels provide a route to test with non-followers [P03].
- **Expected impact:** A portfolio-level conversion lift from the observed 1.28 net follows per 1,000 account views toward 2.0-2.6 in the strong path.
- **Required effort:** 2 hours weekly for variants, logging, and a 30-minute decision review.
- **Execution:** Test one variable at a time, score at equal age, keep a hook/mechanism library, and give winners 3-5 more executions. Repurpose clean winner exports to TikTok and Shorts, but report native follower results separately.
- **KPI:** Follower efficiency index, number of repeat winners per month, and monthly net follows per 1,000 Instagram views.

### Bet 4: Borrow aligned audiences through art, fashion, and movement Collabs

- **Evidence:** Existing studio Collabs concentrated Danish reach but converted only 0.44-0.76 follows per 1,000 views, suggesting shared-post mechanics alone are insufficient [A07]. Instagram supports joint Collab distribution [P05], and Copenhagen offers relevant design, fashion, art, and community partners [K01-K12].
- **Expected impact:** Wider but still relevant discovery; planning upside of 75-500 incremental Instagram followers per strong activation.
- **Required effort:** 6-12 hours per activation plus a continuous one-hour weekly outreach block.
- **Execution:** Prioritize dancers, movement creators, jewelry/fashion designers, and visual artists. Build the collaboration around a jointly meaningful transformation, require shared publishing and analytics, and reject partners whose audience fit or distribution cannot be verified.
- **KPI:** Incremental follows per activation, follows per 1,000 Collab views, non-follower share, and collaborator-audience contribution.

### Bet 5: Use paid media as an evidence-gated amplifier

- **Evidence:** The highest follower-attributed posts were likely boosted at about EUR 10/day for roughly two weeks, but objective, targeting, paid reach, and organic/paid split are unknown [A08]. The signal justifies a controlled retest, not a claim that boosting caused all 513 follows.
- **Expected impact:** Planning upside of 70-750 incremental Instagram followers per month within the EUR 250-750 test budget if one or more organic winners hold efficient paid conversion.
- **Required effort:** 1-2 hours setup/reporting per test plus brief daily checks.
- **Execution:** Freeze organic results, fund only posts above 2.0 organic follows per 1,000 views, use explicit audiences and exclusions, stop at the half-budget failure gate, and review 30-day retention. Cap paid at 35% of incremental growth unless both paid retention and organic efficiency improve.
- **KPI:** Cost per incremental follower <=EUR 1.50, cost per 30-day retained follower <=EUR 2.50, and no decline in organic follower efficiency.

## Execution order under the weekly time cap

- **Weeks 1-2:** Establish the scorecard and IDs; run experiments 1, 3, 7, and the profile change in 10. Do not spend yet.
- **Weeks 3-4:** Repeat the best hook and format; begin experiments 2, 4, and 11, with experiment 11 limited each week to three posts and 60-90 incremental minutes. Kill weak variants by the relative rules.
- **Weeks 5-8:** Run one high-fit collaboration from 5 or 14, start the Wabbeli series in 9, and use experiment 6 only if an organic creative qualifies.
- **Weeks 9-12:** Increase the volume of no more than two repeat winners; complete experiment 11's ten-week publishing run using shared raw capture; add one medium-priority channel only if weekly core publishing remains on schedule.
